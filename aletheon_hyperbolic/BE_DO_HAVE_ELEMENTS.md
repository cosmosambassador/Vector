## BE, DO and HAVE

| Stage | Practical question | Required record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BE | Who is responsible, what is the purpose, and what is already known? | Identity and ownership; research scope; prior evidence; assumptions; authorization |
| DO | What action will answer the question, and how will it be checked? | Method, implementation, bounded experiment, acceptance criteria and execution results |
| HAVE | What result now exists, and what does it support? | Traceable artifact, measured outcome, limitations, pending review and permitted use |

A result may be a failure, an unresolved question, a document, source code, or a trained checkpoint. A passing test supports the behavior it checks. Claims of physical discovery, predictive accuracy or institutional approval each require their own evidence.

## GitHub and Hugging Face handoff

For this project, GitHub carries the engineering and review record. A future Hugging Face release would carry the selected model artifact and its accompanying evidence. Neither platform's presence proves model quality.

1. **BE:** identify the model owner, task, data provenance, license and intended use.
2. **DO:** train reproducibly; assess checkpoint integrity and finite parameters; evaluate on an appropriate untouched final dataset; record baseline comparisons and limitations.
3. **HAVE:** assemble the checkpoint, file hash, configuration, exact source commit, evaluation report and model card as a reviewable release candidate.
4. **Human decision:** verify publication is authorized for the destination and artifact before uploading weights. Record the resulting release version and links if publication occurs.

This framework creates no checkpoint, Hugging Face upload, persistent autonomous agent, or new platform capability.

## Fixed 30-foot circumference

Founder clarification: “Circumference 60,” followed by “30 feet no matter distance or speed or direction.” TJ then authorized recording the proposed fixed-circumference interpretation with “Do it.” The current design value is **30 feet around**. The earlier 60 is retained here as a superseded value with unspecified units; 33 inches remains an unassigned dimension. It has not been established as a height.

For the proposed geometric model, define C as the perimeter of the chosen cross-section measured in the model's own local frame:

```text
C = 30 ft = 9.144 m
C(distance traveled, speed, direction) = 30 ft
```

This is a stipulated design constraint: translation and rotation preserve the modeled cross-section, with no motion-dependent deformation included. It does not equate circumference with travel distance or impose a speed. A physical realization would require a specified structure, measurement procedure and dynamics. Measurements in other reference frames are outside this specification.

If the cross-section is circular:

| Quantity | Definition | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Circumference | C | 30 ft |
| Diameter | C / pi | approximately 9.549297 ft |
| Radius | C / (2 pi) | approximately 4.774648 ft |

The circumference alone does not require a circular cross-section or determine its shape, height, surface area or volume. The diameter and radius above apply only under the circular assumption. No relationship to the five symbolic positions is assigned.
